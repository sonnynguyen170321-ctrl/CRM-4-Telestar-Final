## 2. Mission Control & Recovery Operations

When a campaign is flagged by the **Delivery Guardian** as `WATCH`, `AT_RISK`, or `CRITICAL`:
1. **Inspect Root Cause**: Check whether the primary constraint is `Lead Inventory Exhaustion`, `Reply SLA Breach`, or `Mailbox Health`.
2. **Review Trade-Off Recovery Options**:
   - `Option A`: Accelerate ICP Sourcing (+200 contacts) -> Requires Leadgen Manager sign-off.
   - `Option B`: Reallocate Eligible Relationship Capital (65 warm contacts) -> Requires Floor Manager sign-off.
   - `Option C`: Enforce 2h Positive Reply SLA -> Requires Team Lead floor enforcement.
   - `Option D`: Combined Recovery Mission (A + B + C) -> Requires Director sign-off.
3. **Execute AI Mission**: Transition Mission status from `PROPOSED` to `APPROVED`.
4. **Record Downstream Outcomes**: Outcome logged in `DecisionLedger` to improve future recommendations.
