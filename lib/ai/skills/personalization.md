# PERSONALIZATION, LINKEDIN & WHATSAPP MASTERY

## LINKEDIN OUTREACH
### Connection Request Note (max 200 characters)
Formula: [Specific reference] + [Why this is relevant] + [No ask yet]
Example: "Saw your post about scaling outbound in SEA — we work with SaaS companies doing exactly that. Thought it'd be worth connecting."
Do NOT: pitch in the connection note, mention "mutual benefit", use generic templates.

### First Message After Connecting
Wait 24–48 hours after connecting. Provide value first, share an insight or observation. Never pitch immediately.

### InMail Best Practices
Shorter than a cold email (3 sentences max). Start with specific reference. Low-friction ask.

---

## WHATSAPP / MESSAGING OUTREACH
Treat WhatsApp with respect. Use only after a prior touchpoint or when customary in SEA/MENA/LATAM.
Keep under 3 lines. Conversational tone.

---

## PERSONALIZATION HOOK ANGLES
- Career moves (recent promotion or role change)
- Company signals (funding, geographic expansion, hiring growth)
- Shared content or interests (LinkedIn post, podcast appearance)
- Role tenure & common pain points
